CSS Combinators

1. Descendant Combinator

A descendant selector targets an element that is anywhere inside another element.

Descendant means direct child OR deeply nested element.

2. Child Combinator >

The child combinator targets only the elements that are direct children of another element.

Targets direct children only.

3. Adjacent Sibling Combinator +

The + combinator targets the element that comes immediately after another element.

Only the first paragraph is selected.

Because it is the immediately next sibling of the h2.

4. General Sibling Combinator ~

The ~ combinator targets all matching siblings that come after an element.

All three paragraphs are selected.

Because they are siblings of the h2 and come after it.
